# Scribly 1.5.0

Meeting recording, transcripts and editable AI notes for your Windows notebook, with clearer navigation and progress feedback.

## What’s new

- Record meetings or import a recording, then create a transcript and meeting summary, action items and key points using your connected services.
- Edit generated meeting notes and speaker names, and use source time links to check the original transcript or recording.
- Ask follow-up questions in the Meeting panel. Answers appear below the question without changing your summary note.
- Create study notes, quizzes and flashcards from a meeting. Each AI action now shows what is running, available transcript-part progress and cancellation feedback. Live drafts are available under Preview draft.
- Improve meeting options, input focus borders and the suggestions toolbar icon.
- Refine note options, Settings and panel transitions, with reduced-motion support. Sidebar ordering retains manual placement alongside sorting.

## Install or update

Windows 10/11 x64: export a portable notebook backup and any meeting recordings you want to keep, close Scribly, then run `Scribly_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe`. Existing notebooks remain under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.still.notes\database`. Updates are installed manually.

PostgreSQL is bundled. Missing WebView2 may require internet access during installation. This installer is unsigned, so Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning. Verify the download with `SHA256SUMS-release.txt`.

Meeting recording and connected AI are available in the Windows app. Transcription and analysis require the relevant connected service; provider limits and charges may apply. Review the account and data-use information in Meeting AI settings before sending recordings or transcripts. Notebook backups do not include meeting recordings and transcripts; back those up separately from Meetings.

Scribly is free and MIT licensed. Bundled software, fonts, data and other third-party content retain their own licenses.
